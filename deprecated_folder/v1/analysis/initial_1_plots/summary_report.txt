 FOOTNOTES
==============================================================================================================
[1] COVERAGE METHODOLOGY: Two coverage metrics are reported. 'Raw Cov' (ungated) measures line coverage for all test outcomes except TIMEOUT, SYNTAX_ERROR, and NO_CODE — even assertion-failing tests report actual coverage, reflecting how much of the solution code the test exercised before failing. 'Gated Cov' measures coverage restricted to assertion-passing tasks only (our Pass@1 criterion). Note: Wang et al. (2025a)'s 'Overall Line Coverage' (marked [a] in Table 1) applies a more lenient gating condition (Execution Correctness: test compiles and runs without crashing) rather than assertion passing; it is therefore not directly equivalent to our Gated Cov but is placed in the same column as the closest analogue. Both our metrics appear in Table 1.

[2] PYNGUIN TPS REPRESENTATION: Pynguin does not generate tokens; it uses evolutionary search (SBST/AST mutations). Pynguin is excluded from TPS trend plots. For cross-paradigm efficiency comparison, refer to the Wall-Clock Efficiency figure.

LITERATURE BASELINES: Results for five reference models are drawn from two independent studies that evaluated on the TestEval benchmark. Metric definitions differ between sources:
  Wang et al. (2025a) — GPT-4o, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct:
    Pass@1 column reports their 'Execution Correctness' (EC): the fraction of tasks where the generated test compiles and runs to completion without an unhandled exception. EC does NOT require assertions to pass; it is therefore more lenient than our assertion-based Pass@1. Values are marked [a].
    Gated Cov column reports their 'Overall Line Coverage': average line coverage measured over EC-passing tasks. Because the gating criterion is EC (not assertion passing), this metric is more lenient than our Gated Cov and is marked [a]. Mutation Score was not reported by Wang et al. (2025a) on TestEval.
  Huang et al. (2025b) — Phi-4-mini-Instruct, DeepSeek-Coder-1.3B-Instruct, Gemma-3-4B-IT:
    Pass@1 column reports their 'Pass@1': fraction of tasks where the generated test passes all assertions. This is assertion-based and directly comparable to our Pass@1.
    Gated Cov column reports their 'LCov@1': average line coverage on assertion-passing tasks, directly comparable to our Gated Cov.
    Mut Score reports their 'Mut@1': mutation score on TestEval (Table 5), comparable to ours.
  Shared context: all baselines used the same TestEval benchmark (same tasks, same reference solutions). Inference details (engine, hardware, sampling) may differ from our setup (vLLM, RTX 4090, T=0.0).

[4] STATISTICAL METHODOLOGY: All pairwise comparisons use Welch's t-test (unequal variances). P-values are Bonferroni-corrected for multiple comparisons. Effect sizes reported as Cohen's h (appropriate for proportions): |h|<0.2=negligible, 0.2-0.5=small, 0.5-0.8=medium, ≥0.8=large. 95% confidence intervals use t-distribution (N=1 runs, t-critical=inf, df=0).

[5] N=1 RUNS JUSTIFICATION: 1 independent runs per configuration balances statistical rigor with computational cost. With N=1: (1) 95% CIs use t-critical value of inf (df=0); (2) Bootstrap analysis confirms ranking stability across resampling. The variance convergence table demonstrates diminishing returns for additional runs.

[6] BOOTSTRAP STABILITY: Rankings assessed via 1000 bootstrap iterations resampling runs with replacement. 'Modal Rank' = most frequent rank position. 'Rank Stability' = percentage of iterations where configuration achieved its modal rank. High stability (>70%) indicates robust rankings.

[7] BEST CONFIGURATION SELECTION: For Tables 4-7, 'best configuration' for each model is defined as the configuration (prompt mode + temperature) with the highest mean Pass@1 across N=1 runs, excluding configurations with zero variance (which cannot be used in statistical tests). This reduces comparisons from all temperature variants to one representative per model.

EXPERIMENTAL SETUP (TestEval Study):
• Hardware: vLLM, Single RTX 4090, FP16 precision (INT4 for quantized) 4096 max tokens.
• Baseline: Literature baselines from Wang et al. (2025a) and Huang et al. (2025b) for comparison.
• Dataset: 'TestEval' - 210 programming problems from TestEval benchmark.
• Definitions:
  - Pass@1: % of tasks passing assertions.
  - Raw Cov: Ungated line coverage (all tasks incl. assertion failures, excl. timeouts/syntax errors/no-code). Gated Cov: Assertion-passing tasks only.
  - Total Tokens: Average total tokens generated per run.
