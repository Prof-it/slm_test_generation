# `objectives.md`

*   To systematically evaluate the performance of open-source Small Language Models (SLMs, <7B parameters) for generating Python unit tests.
*   To determine if SLMs are a viable and cost-effective alternative to Large Language Models (LLMs) for AI-driven testing in software development.
*   To analyze how model architecture, prompting strategies (single-step, two-step, agentic), and hyperparameters (temperature) influence the quality of generated tests.
*   To produce the first targeted benchmark comparing modern SLMs for Python unit test generation, addressing a key gap in current research.
